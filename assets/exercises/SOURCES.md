# Exercise photo sources

Photographic movement references are bundled locally from the public-domain [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) (Unlicense; see LICENSE.md). Each filename retains its source exercise ID; -0 and -1 are the two reference positions.

Six supplementary photos in movement-sheet.png were generated specifically for the step-up, Spiderman forearm plank, kneeling oblique slam, reverse lunge with curl, wide cable row, and burpee jump. They are illustrative references; the written guide describes the full movement.

The five poses in stretch-reference.png are AI-generated illustrations based on the user's podiatrist handout, including straight- and bent-knee calf stretches. Follow the accompanying instructions and the podiatrist's guidance.

Spider_Crawl shows the knee-to-elbow push-up position used by the Spiderman push-up. Dumbbell_Lunges shows one walking lunge step. The generated burpee picture shows the jump phase; its guide includes the push-up.
